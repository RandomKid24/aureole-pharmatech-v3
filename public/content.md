## 3. Product Catalog Specifications

### Walk-In Environmental Systems
- **Walk In BOD Incubator**: Precise and uniform temperature conditions inside the chamber.
  - *Temperature Range*: 10°C to 60°C
  - *Control Accuracy*: ± 0.2°C
- **Walk In Humidity / Stability Chamber**: For large-scale testing.
- **Walk In Cold Chamber**: Temperature-controlled cold room environments.

### Stability Chambers & Ovens
- **Humidity / Stability Chamber**: Precise environmental control for long-term stability studies and ICH compliance.
  - *Temperature Range*: 20°C to 60°C
  - *Humidity Range*: 40% to 95% RH
- **Ultra Low Deep Freezer**: Ultra-low temperature storage for biological samples and vaccines.
  - *Temperature Range*: Up to -80°C
  - *Control System*: PLC Based
- **Muffle Furnance**: Designed for high-temperature material testing.
  - *Temperature Range*: Up to 1150°C
  - *Control Accuracy*: ± 5°C

### Table Top & Laboratory Instruments
- **Eco Series (Ultrasonic Bath)**: High-efficiency ultrasonic cleaning solutions.
  - *Method*: Ultrasonic cavitation
  - *Control Interface*: Analog/Digital
- **Steam Bath**: Water bath maintaining 100°C for microbiology and testing.
  - *Temperature Range*: Ambient + 5°C to 100°C
  - *Control Accuracy*: ± 2°C
- **Constant Water Bath**: Heated cabinet for sensitive specimen incubation.
  - *Temperature Range*: Ambient + 5°C to 99.9°C
  - *Control Accuracy*: ± 0.5°C
- **Oil Bath**: High-temperature immersion bath.
  - *Temperature Range*: 50°C to 250°C
  - *Control Accuracy*: ± 2°C
- **Air Sampler (Basic 4)**: Environmental monitoring and cleanroom air quality testing.
  - *Flow Rate*: 25 to 200 Litres/minute
  - *Sampling Volume*: 25 to 2000 Litres

### Laboratory Furniture
- **Modular Laboratory Furniture**: Ergonomic, robust lab benches and workstations.
- **Stainless Steel Accessories**: High-grade SS furniture and accessories for cleanroom compliance.
